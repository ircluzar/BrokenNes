Deck Builder Welcome

Welcome to BrokenNes

In Deck Builder, you will iteratively build up a custom deck by swapping components and playing bits of videogames.

As you progress, Cores will be unlocked in card packs after completing achievements. On each level, you may be forced to use new Cores before they can be unlocked. The Cores that do not have an enforced card can be customized from your Deck.

BrokenNes uses savestates as you get inside and outside of the games. Changing cores can sometimes make your savestates break. For an optimal leveling up experience, prioritize Easy achievements and switching games.

Thank you for playing BrokenNes. Embrace the jank.
