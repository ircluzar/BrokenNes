ℹ️ How to Use

  • Music: Click "Play" to start immediately, "Request" to crossfade from current track
  • SFX: Click any SFX to play it instantly (multiple can play at once)
  • Crossfade: Use "Request" on a different track while music is playing to hear the smooth transition
  • Volume: Adjust music and SFX volumes independently
  • Loop: Toggle whether music should loop continuously
