The BrokenNes NES sound chip as a synth for web pages. Browsers keep a page silent until you interact with it, so:

BrokenNes Nano

A NES sound chip in a web page: two pulse channels, a triangle and a noise channel, sample for sample the chip behind the BrokenNes2 FL Studio plugin.

Chip

Instrument

Keyboard

Play with the mouse or the computer keyboard: ZSXDCVGBHNJM (low row) and Q2W3ER5T6Y7UI (high row). Hold two notes on a pulse instrument and they use both pulse channels; a third takes the oldest channel and gives it back.

Sound effects (one line each: synth.play('coin'))

Tune player: a 16-step pattern, scheduled ahead of time on the audio clock

Click cells to edit the drums. The triangle channel is shared by the bass and the kick, the noise channel by the snare and the hats: when two collide the newer sound takes the channel, as on the console.
