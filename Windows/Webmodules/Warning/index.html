Health & Safety Warning

⚡ PHOTOSENSITIVE SEIZURE WARNING

A very small percentage of people may experience a seizure when exposed to certain visual images, including flashing lights or patterns that may appear in video games and emulated content. Even people who have no history of seizures or epilepsy may have an undiagnosed condition that can cause these "photosensitive epileptic seizures" while using this emulator.

Potential Health Risks

  • Flashing Images: The emulator may display rapidly flashing lights, patterns, or images that could trigger seizures in susceptible individuals.
  • Loud Audio: Sudden loud noises or audio glitches may occur during emulation, which could be harmful to hearing or startling.
  • Visual Distortion: Shader effects and visual corruptions may produce unexpected patterns or effects.
  • Extended Use: Prolonged use may cause eye strain, headaches, or fatigue.

Symptoms to Watch For

  • Dizziness or lightheadedness
  • Altered vision or disorientation
  • Eye or muscle twitching
  • Loss of awareness or confusion
  • Involuntary movements or convulsions
🛑 DO NOT USE THIS SOFTWARE IF:

You or anyone in your family has a history of seizures or epilepsy. Immediately discontinue use and consult a physician if you experience any of the symptoms listed above.

Recommendations for Safe Use

• Take regular breaks (at least 15 minutes every hour)
• Use in a well-lit room
• Keep a safe distance from the screen
• Adjust volume to comfortable levels
• Avoid using when tired or fatigued
• If you experience discomfort, stop immediately
